### 5.1 Technical Capability (what exists and how it is verified)

Renwick Systems' capability is a working production system, not a design. Project Legacy is a family application and a
companion staff application, both live on the App Store and Google Play (iOS 1.0.2 approved 2026-10-23; Android 1.0.2
published the same day), built end to end by one engineer in sixteen weeks and operated by one person.

Its architecture is the capability we propose to carry into a defense or enterprise setting:

1. **Symbolic layer: engines that compute, never guess.** Deterministic chart engines (blueprint_engine.py,
   epiccycle_engine.py, resolution_engine.py, family_mesh_engine.py) compute every value from the household's
   own input data. A value the engines cannot compute raises or returns empty; the codebase bans defaults, placeholders
   and fallback charts outright (CLAUDE.md rules 1-4), and `date.today()` is never read inside a computation: time is a
   parameter.
2. **Independent verification, by construction.** `evaluation/independent_chart_oracle.py` recomputes every core value
   from the published rules in code that shares nothing with the engines ("Consistency is not authenticity ... Where the two
   disagree, the disagreement is the finding"). Run on 2026-11-09: 968 of 968 values independently confirmed, 0
   mismatches; `evaluation/engine_live_verification.py` 130 of 130 checks (every value varies across inputs; a constant is
   not reported as a signal, rule 6). A second oracle, `way_through` in the same module, re-solves the engine's computed
   resolution by a different algorithm; engine and twin agreed on 1,500 of 1,500 wide-population dyads when it landed.
3. **The language model words the result; it does not produce it.** Every model is a LoRA adapter on one open base
   (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct; teacher qwen/qwen3.8-27b, Apache-2.0, chosen so the outputs are owned outright). The adapter
   is handed the whole computed chart and asked to write; "nothing but the calculations stands between the engines and a
   model" (CLAUDE.md rule 11). Prompts are assembled in exactly one place for training and serving (rule 7), so the served
   shape is the trained shape; the frame is pinned by test (tests/test_stock_frame.py).
4. **Every invariant is executable** (rule 5). over 300 test files under tests/; release gates that refuse a dirty tree, a failed container
   test, a drifted prompt, an unpinned model location (`model_registry.py` is the one place a model is named; the serving
   manifest gate fails on any copy). The deploy pipeline, the ADRs (docs/adr/ADR-001..003) and the postmortems are in the
   repository.
5. **Measured, not asserted, reading of the symbolic input.** On 2026-11-01 a chart-swap harness
   (chart_swap_pairs.py, chart_swap_score.py) measured, for each of nine adapters, how much the written text depends on the
   chart it was given (teacher-forced NLL with the true chart vs a swapped one, 120 held-out rows a product). Every adapter
   read its chart 2.0 to 5.6 times more than the bare base model; the reading runs through the chart's worded values
   (90-96% of the effect), not the bare numbers. The 27B teacher on the same rows scored 0.299 vs the adapter's 0.229.
   These are the numbers we state; they are advisory, kept in evaluation/reports/chart_swap_2026-11-01.md.
6. **Operated for a few hundred dollars a month by one person.** Cloud Run scale-to-zero, one GPU service carrying eleven
   adapters, one worker job on a Postgres queue, daily and weekly routines that read health, service levels and the bill
   (the idle run rate read 2026-11-09: about $16-17 a day).

What we do NOT claim: that the model's prose is checked at serving time. By the owner's ruling (rule 11) nothing judges a
model's words between the model and the family; the two gates that exist (`evaluation/content_gates.py`) check only that
a surface can display the text and that it meets the App Store and Google Play content policies. Fidelity of the words to
the computed facts is measured offline (item 5) and improved by training, never filtered at the output. A proposal that
needs output-time checking (the Navy topic's "traceable references for all generated outputs") would add that as new work,
honestly scoped, not as something already built.

### 5.2 Innovation

The innovation is a production neurosymbolic architecture in which the symbolic side holds ALL of the authority over
facts and the neural side holds none, and in which that boundary is enforced by executable checks rather than by prompt
instructions:

- **Symbolic-first dataflow.** Inputs arrive already symbolic (structured records), the engines compute every derived
  value and the way through a conflict, and the adapter receives the complete computed block. The field's consensus is
  symbolic authority over neural output (knowledge_banks/NeurosymboicAI.txt, chapter 1, "What Is Neurosymbolic AI? An
  Overview and Frontier Problems": neurosymbolic AI "as a foundation for enabling" trustworthy and efficient models);
  the project's contribution is to make the authority total and to prove it with an oracle that shares no code with the
  engines (rule 8).
- **An independent oracle as the verification primitive.** Not a confidence score, not a second model: a recomputation
  from the published rules. This is the pattern the Navy topic asks for in other words ("transparent and auditable
  reasoning pathways"): the reasoning pathway is the engine's, it is deterministic, and the oracle audits it.
- **Measured reading instead of asserted grounding.** The chart-swap contrast (5.1 item 5) is a falsifiable measure of
  whether a trained adapter reads its symbolic input at all, and of which parts it reads. It found that the numbers alone
  carry almost nothing and the worded values carry the reading, which is itself a finding about how to hand symbols to a
  language model (knowledge_banks/Neuro-Symbolic AI book3.txt, chapter 6 "A Marriage of Neurons and Symbols: Opportunities
  and Obstacles", sections "Knowledge and symbolic representation" and "Transparency and interpretability").
- **Coverage-designed training data.** The corpus population is drawn so that every value of every symbolic field is seen
  early (dyad_scenes/wide_population.py, "any prefix of the order is the best-covering set of its size"); a student learns
  the word for a value only from rows that carry it.
- **Owned outright.** Open base, Apache-2.0 teacher, every adapter, corpus and engine the company's own; no hosted
  provider's terms over the trained artefacts (the owner's ruling 2026-10-23).

For the Navy topic the Phase I question becomes: can the same architecture ingest an organization's authoritative
documents as the symbolic layer (retrieval that returns the citation with the fact, an oracle that checks the quoted
passage exists and says what the model says it says), with a small adapter wording the answer and the measured
chart-swap-style contrast proving the answer depends on the retrieved authority? That is a feasibility study we can scope
and price, and the parts that are new (document ingestion, the citation oracle, the output-time traceability) are named
as new.

## 5.4 DSIP pre-release questions for DON27TZ02-NV012 (drafted 2026-11-09 for the owner to post; his hand)

DSIP topic Q&A is open during pre-release (Nov 04 to Dec 02, 2026) and posted publicly with the answers; questions are
anonymous to other proposers but carry no company information in any case. Each question below is grounded in a line of the
topic text and in what the Phase I proposal would need to know. Post the ones he wants, in his words; one question a post.

1. **Sample corpora.** Phase I is "built on sample Navy corpora" and names NETC curricula, qualification standards, MyNavy HR
   competency frameworks and technical manuals. Will the Government furnish representative unclassified, distribution-A
   samples of these document types for Phase I, or should proposers plan to use publicly available Navy MPTE documents
   (e.g. public NAVEDTRA materials) as stand-ins?
2. **Scope of "hyper-parameterized LLM fine-tuning".** Does the Navy intend fine-tuning of a base model's weights (e.g. a
   LoRA adapter), prompt-side specialization over a frozen foundation model, or either, so long as outputs are grounded and
   traceable? Is a small open-weights base (7-8B class) acceptable for Phase I feasibility, or is an enterprise foundation
   model expected?
3. **Explainability bar.** "Transparent and auditable reasoning pathways" and "traceable references to source
   documentation" are required. Is a citation-per-claim to the retrieved passage, plus a deterministic check that the cited
   passage exists and supports the claim, the intended form of explainability, or does the Navy expect a natural-language
   rationale generated by the model as well?
4. **Hallucination measurement.** Phase I must "develop and validate an initial approach for reducing hallucination". Does
   the Navy have a preferred metric or benchmark (e.g. a held-out question set with gold citations, attribution precision,
   abstention rate on unanswerable questions), or should proposers define and justify the measure?
5. **Deployment boundary.** The keywords include "zero-trust AI deployment" and "federated learning". For Phase I, is an
   on-premises or Government-cloud deployment design (no data leaving the Navy enclave, no third-party hosted model) the
   expectation, and is federated learning a Phase I requirement or a Phase II consideration?
6. **Competency standards.** Phase II names the DCWF, Navy KSATs, xAPI and IEEE 1484.20.3. Should the Phase I architecture
   already show how a response maps to a competency definition, or is the Phase I deliverable limited to the ingestion
   concept, the fine-tuning/RAG architecture and the hallucination-reduction approach?
7. **Research institution role.** For the STTR partner, does the Navy have a preference between a university's learning
   science / instructional design group and a computer science group (retrieval and evaluation), given the topic spans both?
8. **Evaluation users.** Will Phase I have access to Navy training SMEs or end users for feedback on the feasibility
   demonstration, or is Phase I evaluation expected to be internal to the proposer and the partner?
9. **Data handling.** Are there restrictions on the proposer using commercial LLM APIs during Phase I research (for example,
   for synthetic question generation) when the Navy sample corpora themselves are not sent to any outside service?
10. **CMMC Level 2 (Self).** Is the self-assessment required at proposal submission or at award for Phase I?

What our answer leans on, so the questions are not idle: our existing path is a deterministic symbolic layer that
produces every fact, an independent oracle that recomputes it, and a small open adapter that only words the result
(section 5.1). For this topic the symbolic layer would be the authoritative documents and their retrieval, the oracle a
citation check, and the measure a swap contrast (does the answer change when the retrieved authority changes). Questions
2, 3 and 4 decide whether that shape is what the Navy means.
